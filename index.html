About CHARUSAT Changa

Charotar University of Science and Technology (CHARUSAT) is established under the Gujarat Act No. 8 of 2009. The campus is spread across 120 acres at Changa, Anand. With state-of-the-art labs, strong industry placement tie-ups, and UGC recognition, CHARUSAT offers advanced B.Tech, MBA, MCA, Pharmacy, and Applied Sciences programs.
